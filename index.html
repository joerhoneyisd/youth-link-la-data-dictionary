Youth Link LA – Data Dictionary

How to fill in the program import spreadsheet. Use one row per program per location. This version replaces the earlier Data Entry Key. Updated November 2026.

Rules that apply everywhere

Separators

Use ; (semicolon) between multiple values. Use | (pipe) between the parts of a structured value, and keep every pipe even when a part is blank.

Blank and “N/A”

“N/A” is only valid where a field says so (Transportation Assistance, Required Documents, Medi-Cal / Insurance). Everywhere else, leave the cell blank.

Filterable fields

Service Category, Minimum Age, Maximum Age, Priority Populations, SPA and Service Languages power search filters. A blank filterable field means the row only appears when nothing is selected in that filter. To appear under every selection, list every value. For ages, blank means no limit.

7 TAY-Friendly Description
Required
 A short, plain-language description a young person can understand. One paragraph. Career and life-skills support for emerging adults. Line breaks; schedules or sub-program names inside the description.
8 Minimum Age
OptionalFilterable
Whole number 0–26: the youngest age of the person who applies or participates (not the end recipient of an item). Leave blank for no lower limit. 18 0 to mean “no minimum”; “N/A”.
9 Maximum Age
OptionalFilterable
Whole number 0–26: the oldest age of the person who applies or participates. Leave blank for no upper limit. Must not be lower than Minimum Age. 25 99, 100 or 101 to mean “no limit”; “N/A”.
10 Priority Program Populations
OptionalFilterable
One or more values from the Priority Populations list, separated by ;. If the program is open to all, list all seven. Blank rows only appear when no population is selected. Foster Youth; Pregnant or Parenting “Open to everyone”; “N/A”; “Justice Involved” without the hyphen.
11 Eligibility Criteria
Optional

Fields with “No required format” accept any text. The example only shows one way to write it. Required means the cell must be filled in. Conditional means it depends on the program, as described.
